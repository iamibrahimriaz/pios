# Research Checklist

---

# Overview

This checklist is the final verification step before research is delivered.

It ensures that nothing important has been overlooked and that the research is complete, consistent, and actionable.

A project should not be considered complete until every applicable item has been reviewed.

---

# How to Use This Checklist

Use this checklist:

- Before submitting research.
- Before handing work to engineering.
- Before presenting to stakeholders.
- Before publishing documentation.
- During self-review.
- During peer review.

If an item does not apply to the project, mark it as **Not Applicable (N/A)** and briefly explain why.

---

# Section 1 — Product Understanding

Confirm that you understand the product.

- [ ] The product idea is clearly defined.
- [ ] The business objective is understood.
- [ ] The scope is documented.
- [ ] Success criteria are defined.
- [ ] Known constraints are documented.
- [ ] Open questions are identified.

---

# Section 2 — Problem Definition

Confirm that the problem has been researched.

- [ ] The primary problem is clearly described.
- [ ] Root causes have been investigated.
- [ ] User pain points are documented.
- [ ] Existing solutions are understood.
- [ ] The problem has been validated.

---

# Section 3 — User Research

Verify user understanding.

- [ ] Primary users are identified.
- [ ] Secondary users are identified.
- [ ] Buyer personas are understood.
- [ ] User goals are documented.
- [ ] User frustrations are documented.
- [ ] Current workflows are understood.

---

# Section 4 — Market Research

Verify market understanding.

- [ ] Market size has been researched.
- [ ] Industry trends are documented.
- [ ] Growth opportunities are identified.
- [ ] Relevant regulations are reviewed.
- [ ] Market risks are identified.

---

# Section 5 — Competitor Research

Verify competitor analysis.

- [ ] Direct competitors are identified.
- [ ] Indirect competitors are identified.
- [ ] Features have been compared.
- [ ] Pricing has been reviewed.
- [ ] Competitive advantages are documented.
- [ ] Market gaps are identified.

---

# Section 6 — Business Validation

Verify business viability.

- [ ] Target customers are defined.
- [ ] Revenue model is identified.
- [ ] Pricing strategy is considered.
- [ ] Business risks are documented.
- [ ] Long-term sustainability is evaluated.

---

# Section 7 — Product Strategy

Verify product planning.

- [ ] Value proposition is defined.
- [ ] MVP scope is identified.
- [ ] Feature priorities are established.
- [ ] Success metrics are defined.
- [ ] Product roadmap is outlined.

---

# Section 8 — Technical Planning

Verify technical readiness.

- [ ] Platform requirements are documented.
- [ ] Architecture approach is selected.
- [ ] Database considerations are documented.
- [ ] API requirements are identified.
- [ ] Integration requirements are understood.
- [ ] Scalability considerations are documented.

---

# Section 9 — Security & Compliance

Verify security planning.

- [ ] Sensitive data has been identified.
- [ ] Authentication requirements are defined.
- [ ] Authorization requirements are defined.
- [ ] Privacy requirements are considered.
- [ ] Regulatory considerations are documented.
- [ ] Backup and recovery considerations exist.

---

# Section 10 — AI Assessment

If AI is part of the product:

- [ ] AI solves a real problem.
- [ ] AI provides measurable value.
- [ ] AI limitations are documented.
- [ ] Required data is identified.
- [ ] Risks of AI are documented.

If AI is not required, document the reasoning.

---

# Section 11 — Risk Assessment

Verify risks.

- [ ] Business risks are documented.
- [ ] Technical risks are documented.
- [ ] User risks are documented.
- [ ] Operational risks are documented.
- [ ] Legal risks are documented.
- [ ] Risk mitigation strategies exist.

---

# Section 12 — Research Quality

Verify research quality.

- [ ] Reliable sources were used.
- [ ] Important claims are supported.
- [ ] Assumptions are identified.
- [ ] Findings are evidence-based.
- [ ] Research is reproducible.
- [ ] Documentation is organized.

---

# Section 13 — Recommendations

Verify recommendations.

- [ ] Recommendations solve identified problems.
- [ ] Alternatives were considered.
- [ ] Trade-offs are documented.
- [ ] Recommendations are practical.
- [ ] Recommendations are prioritized.

---

# Section 14 — Documentation

Verify documentation quality.

- [ ] Executive summary exists.
- [ ] Findings are organized.
- [ ] Terminology is consistent.
- [ ] References are included.
- [ ] Deliverables are complete.
- [ ] Documentation is implementation-ready.

---

# Section 15 — Final Review

Before delivery:

- [ ] No major questions remain unanswered.
- [ ] No unsupported conclusions remain.
- [ ] No obvious contradictions exist.
- [ ] The document has been reviewed.
- [ ] The research meets the Quality Gate.
- [ ] The project is ready for implementation.

---

# Completion Criteria

The checklist is complete when:

- Every applicable item has been reviewed.
- Any incomplete items have explanations.
- Remaining risks are documented.
- Outstanding questions are visible.
- The project passes the Quality Gate.

---

# Quick Review Checklist

Before submission, verify:

✓ Problem understood

✓ Users researched

✓ Market analyzed

✓ Competitors reviewed

✓ Business validated

✓ Technology evaluated

✓ Risks documented

✓ Evidence provided

✓ Recommendations justified

✓ Documentation complete

✓ Quality Gate passed

---

# Self Assessment

Ask yourself:

- If I were the product owner, would this research give me enough confidence to invest in this product?
- If I were the engineer, would I understand what needs to be built?
- If I were the designer, would I understand the user problems?
- If I were the investor, would I understand the opportunity?
- If I were another researcher, could I reproduce this work?

If any answer is **No**, continue improving the research.
