# Research Methodology

---

# Overview

Research is the foundation of every successful product.

Product Intelligence OS follows a structured methodology that transforms an idea into validated product intelligence.

The methodology focuses on understanding reality rather than confirming assumptions.

The objective is not simply to collect information.

The objective is to produce knowledge that enables confident product decisions.

---

# Research Philosophy

Research should always be:

- User-centered
- Evidence-based
- Problem-driven
- Business-aware
- Technology-aware
- Repeatable
- Transparent
- Actionable

Research is considered successful only when another qualified researcher can reproduce similar conclusions using the same methodology.

---

# Research Principles

Every research activity should follow these principles.

## Understand Before Investigating

Do not begin researching until the product idea and objectives are clearly understood.

Research without context produces low-quality results.

---

## Define Research Objectives

Before starting research, clearly define:

- What needs to be learned?
- Why is it important?
- Which decisions depend on this research?
- What constitutes success?

Clear objectives prevent unnecessary research.

---

## Research the Problem, Not the Solution

Never begin by validating a preferred solution.

Instead investigate:

- The actual problem.
- Existing workflows.
- Current pain points.
- User behavior.
- Business needs.

Good solutions emerge from understanding problems.

---

## Follow Evidence

Allow evidence to guide conclusions.

Do not ignore findings because they conflict with expectations.

If research disproves an assumption, update the assumption.

---

# Research Lifecycle

Every research project should follow the same lifecycle.

```
Define Objective

↓

Understand Context

↓

Prepare Research Plan

↓

Collect Information

↓

Verify Sources

↓

Analyze Findings

↓

Generate Insights

↓

Validate Conclusions

↓

Document Results

↓

Review Quality
```

Each stage builds upon the previous one.

---

# Step 1 — Define Objectives

Identify:

- Research goals
- Expected deliverables
- Scope
- Constraints
- Success criteria

Deliverable:

Research Objective Document

---

# Step 2 — Understand Context

Research should begin by understanding:

- Industry
- Business
- Users
- Existing products
- Market
- Environment

Deliverable:

Context Analysis

---

# Step 3 — Prepare a Research Plan

Create a research plan before gathering information.

The plan should define:

- Topics to investigate
- Information sources
- Research priorities
- Expected outputs
- Timeline

Deliverable:

Research Plan

---

# Step 4 — Collect Information

Gather information from multiple perspectives.

Typical research areas include:

- Market
- Users
- Competitors
- Technology
- Business
- Operations
- Pricing
- Regulations
- Security

Always prioritize reliable sources.

---

# Step 5 — Validate Information

Not all information is trustworthy.

Evaluate every important source based on:

- Accuracy
- Credibility
- Authority
- Relevance
- Timeliness

Cross-check important findings whenever possible.

---

# Step 6 — Analyze Findings

Information becomes valuable only after analysis.

Activities include:

- Pattern recognition
- Comparison
- Categorization
- Gap analysis
- SWOT analysis
- Root cause analysis

Deliverable:

Research Analysis

---

# Step 7 — Generate Insights

Research should produce insights rather than summaries.

An insight should answer:

- Why is this important?
- What does it mean?
- How does it affect the product?
- What opportunity exists?

Deliverable:

Insight Report

---

# Step 8 — Validate Conclusions

Before making recommendations:

Verify:

- Evidence
- Logic
- Assumptions
- Consistency
- Completeness

Do not finalize recommendations until validation is complete.

---

# Step 9 — Document Results

Research documentation should be:

- Structured
- Clear
- Reproducible
- Actionable

Every major finding should include:

- Evidence
- Explanation
- Recommendation
- Confidence level

---

# Step 10 — Review

Perform a final review.

Check:

- Missing information
- Contradictions
- Unsupported conclusions
- Weak assumptions
- Documentation quality

No research should bypass review.

---

# Research Sources

Whenever possible, prioritize information in the following order:

1. Official documentation
2. Government publications
3. Industry reports
4. Academic research
5. Product documentation
6. Customer interviews
7. User feedback
8. Community discussions
9. AI-generated analysis (supporting only)

AI-generated content should never replace reliable evidence.

---

# Research Techniques

Depending on the product, use techniques such as:

- Market Research
- Competitor Analysis
- User Interviews
- Customer Journey Mapping
- SWOT Analysis
- Root Cause Analysis
- JTBD Analysis
- Feature Comparison
- Gap Analysis
- Trend Analysis

Choose techniques appropriate to the research objective.

---

# Research Documentation Standards

Every research document should include:

- Objective
- Scope
- Methodology
- Findings
- Insights
- Evidence
- Recommendations
- Risks
- References
- Confidence Level

This ensures consistency across all research projects.

---

# Common Indicators of Good Research

Research is considered high quality when it is:

- Complete
- Logical
- Evidence-based
- Transparent
- Reproducible
- Well-structured
- Actionable

Good research enables confident decision-making.

---

# Self Assessment

Before completing research, ask:

- Did I answer the original objective?
- Did I investigate the real problem?
- Did I verify important information?
- Did I gather multiple perspectives?
- Are recommendations supported by evidence?
- Can another researcher understand my reasoning?
- Is the documentation implementation-ready?

If any answer is **No**, continue improving the research.
