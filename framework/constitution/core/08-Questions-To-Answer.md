# Questions To Answer

---

# Overview

Good product research is driven by good questions.

Poor questions produce incomplete research.

Excellent questions uncover hidden problems, unknown risks, new opportunities, and better solutions.

This document defines the minimum set of questions that every AI agent should answer before delivering any product research.

If important questions remain unanswered, the research should be considered incomplete.

---

# Section 1 — Understanding the Idea

Before researching anything, answer:

- What is the product idea?
- Why does this idea exist?
- What problem is being solved?
- Who proposed the idea?
- What outcome is expected?
- Why is this the right time?

---

# Section 2 — Understanding the Problem

Understand the problem before designing solutions.

Answer:

- What is the actual problem?
- Who experiences it?
- How often does it occur?
- How serious is it?
- What is causing it?
- What happens if it remains unsolved?
- Is this a real problem or only an assumption?

---

# Section 3 — Understanding Users

Research the people behind the product.

Answer:

- Who are the primary users?
- Who are the secondary users?
- Who makes purchasing decisions?
- What are their goals?
- What frustrations do they have?
- How do they currently solve the problem?
- What motivates them to change?

---

# Section 4 — Understanding the Market

Research the environment.

Answer:

- How large is the market?
- Is it growing?
- Which countries are relevant?
- What trends influence this market?
- What opportunities exist?
- What threats exist?
- What regulations affect the market?

---

# Section 5 — Competitor Analysis

Understand existing solutions.

Answer:

- Who are the direct competitors?
- Who are the indirect competitors?
- What are they doing well?
- Where are they weak?
- What features do they provide?
- How do they earn revenue?
- What opportunities remain unserved?

---

# Section 6 — Product Strategy

Understand the future product.

Answer:

- What makes this product different?
- Why would customers choose it?
- What unique value does it provide?
- What should be included in the MVP?
- Which features should be postponed?
- What success metrics will be measured?

---

# Section 7 — Business

Evaluate business viability.

Answer:

- Who pays?
- Why will they pay?
- What pricing model fits?
- What are expected costs?
- Is the business sustainable?
- What competitive advantage exists?

---

# Section 8 — Technology

Understand technical direction.

Answer:

- Which platforms are required?
- Which technologies fit best?
- What integrations are necessary?
- Are there technical constraints?
- How will the system scale?
- What infrastructure is required?

---

# Section 9 — AI Opportunities

Determine whether AI provides meaningful value.

Answer:

- Does AI solve a real problem?
- What manual work can be automated?
- Which AI models fit the use case?
- What data is required?
- What limitations exist?
- What risks should be considered?

Do not recommend AI without measurable value.

---

# Section 10 — Security

Protect users and data.

Answer:

- What sensitive data exists?
- Who can access it?
- How will authentication work?
- What permissions are required?
- What legal requirements exist?
- How will backups be handled?

---

# Section 11 — Scalability

Plan for future growth.

Answer:

- Can the system support more users?
- Can it support more data?
- Can it support multiple regions?
- What bottlenecks may appear?
- What monitoring is required?

---

# Section 12 — Risks

Identify uncertainties.

Answer:

- What could cause failure?
- What assumptions exist?
- What dependencies exist?
- What external risks exist?
- What internal risks exist?
- How can risks be reduced?

---

# Section 13 — Validation

Verify research quality.

Answer:

- What evidence supports the conclusions?
- Which assumptions remain?
- What still needs validation?
- Which recommendations have the highest confidence?
- Which recommendations need further research?

---

# Section 14 — Implementation

Prepare for development.

Answer:

- Is the research complete?
- Can engineering begin?
- Are requirements clear?
- Are priorities defined?
- Is the roadmap realistic?
- Are dependencies documented?

---

# Questions to Always Ask Yourself

Before producing any recommendation, ask:

- Do I fully understand the problem?
- Am I making assumptions?
- Have I researched enough?
- What evidence supports this?
- Is there another approach?
- What risks am I ignoring?
- Have I explained my reasoning?
- Is this recommendation actionable?

These questions should become habitual.

---

# Expected Outcomes

Following this document should ensure that:

- Research is complete.
- Unknowns are minimized.
- Decisions are justified.
- Risks are visible.
- Documentation is implementation-ready.

---

# Self Assessment

Before finishing any research project:

✓ Have all important questions been answered?

✓ Are unanswered questions documented?

✓ Are assumptions clearly identified?

✓ Have recommendations been justified?

✓ Can another researcher reproduce this work?

If any answer is **No**, continue researching.
