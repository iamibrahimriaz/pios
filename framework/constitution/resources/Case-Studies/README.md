# Case Studies Library

---

# Overview

Theory teaches principles.

Experience teaches judgment.

The Case Studies library captures real-world product stories, strategic decisions, product launches, failures, pivots, and innovations that provide practical learning beyond theoretical frameworks.

Every case study should answer not only **what happened**, but also **why it happened**, **what can be learned**, and **how those lessons apply to future product decisions**.

---

# Mission

The mission of this library is to transform real-world product experiences into reusable intelligence.

Every case study should strengthen Product Intelligence OS by preserving valuable lessons from actual products and organizations.

---

# Why Case Studies Matter

Great product managers learn from experience.

Great AI should learn from thousands of experiences.

Case studies help AI understand:

- Product evolution
- Market dynamics
- User behavior
- Business strategy
- Technical decisions
- Product failures
- Organizational learning

They bridge the gap between theory and practice.

---

# Objectives

This library enables AI to:

- Analyze successful products.
- Learn from failed products.
- Identify recurring patterns.
- Understand strategic decisions.
- Recognize product evolution.
- Improve future recommendations.

# Standard Structure

Every case study should follow this structure.

```
Title

Executive Summary

Background

Problem

Objectives

Research

Decision Process

Implementation

Results

Lessons Learned

Key Takeaways

References

Related Case Studies
```

Consistency makes the library easier to navigate and compare.

---

# Quality Guidelines

Every case study should be:

- Fact-based
- Well researched
- Objective
- Comprehensive
- Actionable
- Educational

Avoid speculation whenever possible.

---

# Naming Convention

Use descriptive filenames.

Examples:

```
spotify-growth-strategy.md

notion-product-evolution.md

figma-collaboration-model.md

slack-product-market-fit.md

netflix-recommendation-system.md
```

Names should clearly identify the product or topic.

---

# Learning Focus

Every case study should answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What assumptions were made?
- What evidence supported decisions?
- What would be done differently today?
- What reusable lessons exist?

The objective is understanding rather than storytelling.
