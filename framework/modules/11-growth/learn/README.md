# Learn — Growth

---

# What This Layer Is

The human curriculum for the Growth module. An executing agent does not read it.

---

# What the Module Does

It plans how the product reaches, converts and retains users. It produces acquisition
channels, growth loops, a retention model, an onboarding strategy, and expansion paths.

Its gate has four criteria and one of them is unusually specific: **time to first value,
measured in minutes, and stated.** Not "quickly." A number.

---

# The Two Named Tests

**The closure test.** A growth loop's output must feed its own input. If it does not
close, it is a funnel — which is a legitimate thing to have, and should be labeled
honestly. Most things called loops are funnels with an arrow drawn back.

**The payback check.** The implied CAC against the payback ceiling module 06 set. This is
the second of the framework's three arithmetic checks, and a breach means regressing to
module 06 or 07 — not revising the conversion estimate that just produced it.

---

# The Named Failure

**Borrowed growth** — conversion rates, channel performance and viral coefficients taken
from another company's market. The numbers are real; they were measured in a market with
a different switching cost, a different buyer and a different alternative. Module 03's
switching cost is the specific thing that makes them non-transferable.

---

# Where Milestone Zero Ends

This is the fourth and final link in the chain. If module 04 declared a shortfall, this
module **withholds acquisition spend** until the validation resolves it.

> Spending to acquire users for an unvalidated problem is buying evidence you already
> decided not to collect.
