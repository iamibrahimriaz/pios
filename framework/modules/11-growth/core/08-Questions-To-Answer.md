# Questions To Answer

---

# Overview

Almost every question here concerns behavior that has not happened yet. That does not make them
unanswerable — it makes the answers **assumptions**, and the module's value lies in which ones
it commits to testing first.

Questions marked **OPERATOR** cannot be inferred. Questions marked **NEEDS USER** require
contact with real people and are the first thing the first ten conversations should establish.

# 2. Growth Model

| # | Question | If unanswered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 | How does this product fundamentally grow? | Tactics with no motion behind them |
| 2.2 | Why this model and not the obvious alternative? | Cannot be re-examined later |
| 2.3 | What does this model require the product to do? | A motion the product cannot support |
| 2.4 | Does the MVP support it? | Growth planned on deferred capability |
| 2.5 | **What is the CAC ceiling implied by price and payback?** | Any motion looks affordable |
| 2.6 | **What does one unit of the chosen motion cost?** | The comparison cannot be made |
| 2.7 | Does the implied CAC fit under the ceiling? | An unaffordable motion survives the plan |
| 2.8 | **OPERATOR** — what payback period is acceptable? | The ceiling is arbitrary |

> 2.7 is the module's decisive question. Raising the assumed conversion rate until it passes is
> the most common way an unaffordable motion reaches a funding decision.

> The most valuable answer in this module is "we do not know, and here
