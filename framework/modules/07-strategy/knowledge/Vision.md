# Vision

---

# What It Is

The vision from `01-idea`, used here for the one job it can legitimately do: **resolving ties.**

Module 01 wrote it as direction under uncertainty. This module is where the uncertainty actually arrives — two
defensible options, comparable scores, and a choice to make. The vision says which way to lean.

| Legitimate use here | Illegitimate use |
| --- | --- |
| Selecting between two options that scored the same | Justifying a capability the comparison did not favor |
| Deciding a borderline cut when the end-to-end test passes either way | Moving the MVP line outward |
| Ordering two V2 directions of equal evidence | Adding a requirement in `08-product` |
| Framing what the product is for, in the checkpoint | Overriding a ranked problem |

> A vision earns its place by settling a tie. If it is only ever used to justify building more, it is not a vision —
> it is a permission slip.

---

# When It Applies

In Move 2 (Compare) and Move 4 (Cut), as a tiebreaker after the criteria have been scored — never before.

---

# How to Apply It Here

**Score first, then consult it.** The comparison criteria come from the research. The vision applies only to what
remains undecided after they have been applied, and using it earlier lets it override evidence.

**Record when it decided something.** "Options B and C scored equally; B is chosen because it moves toward «vision»"
is an auditable decision. An unrecorded tiebreak is indistinguishable from preference.

**Check the chosen approach against it.** If the winning option leads somewhere the vision does not describe, one of
the two is wrong. That is a genuine finding and it belongs at the human checkpoint.

**Use it to shape non-goals, not to soften them.** A vision can explain *why* something is excluded now —
"consistent with the destination, but not on the path to it". It cannot license inclusion.

**Keep it out of `08-product`.** Requirements trace to ranked problems. A requirement traced to a vision is
`08-product`'s scope laundering, and the boundary check there is designed to catch it.

---

# Where It Misleads

**The vision is the single most effective instrument for scope expansion in the framework**, because every addition
it justifies is individually reasonable. The cumulative effect is an MVP that solves nothing end to end.

**A large vision compensates for a weak premise.** Where `04-problem` declared a shortfall, a compelling vision makes
the strategy read as confident. The shortfall is the fact; the vision is the framing, and only one of them should
propagate.

**It gets rewritten to match the chosen approach.** Once an option is selected, adjusting the vision to fit makes the
tiebreaker circular. If the vision changes, say so and say why.

**Vision is confused with strategy.** A destination is not a route. This module produces the route, and it produces
it from evidence, with the vision breaking ties along the way.

**It becomes the answer at the human checkpoint.** The operator needs the proposal, the non-goals, the load-bearing
assumption and the decisions. A vision presented in their place removes the decision.
