# Alternative Solutions

---

# What It Is

The approaches that do not look like the product anyone set out to build — kept in the option set because they
frequently solve the problem sooner, cheaper, or at all.

| Alternative | When it is the right answer |
| --- | --- |
| **A person doing the work** | Demand is unproven, volume is low, or quality matters more than cost |
| **A template, checklist or form** | The problem is inconsistency rather than effort |
| **A configuration of an existing tool** | The user already owns something that could do this |
| **A change to the process** | The cause is organizational, not technical |
| **An integration only** | The work is fine; the copying between systems is the problem |
| **Doing nothing** | The status quo is adequate and the honest finding is that there is no product |

The last row is a legitimate output of this module. `04-problem`'s stop-and-rethink trigger exists for it, and a run
that reaches it has produced real value.

---

# When It Applies

In Move 1 (Diverge), as at least one of the three options. It is also the sanity check on the chosen approach: if a
form field would work, the product should be a form field.

---

# How to Apply It Here

**Compare each alternative on outcome, not on sophistication.** The question is whether the ranked problem is solved
for the primary persona. A checklist that solves it beats software that partly does.

**Take the human option through the full comparison.** Price it, size it, state its ceiling. It usually loses on
scale and wins on time-to-first-customer, and both belong in Move 2's scoring.

**Check whether the problem is process-shaped.** `04-problem`'s root-cause chain often terminates in an
organizational cause. Where it does, software addresses a symptom — which may still be correct, but only as a
decision.

**Use the alternative as the baseline the chosen approach must beat.** This is `14-ai-systems`' method applied one
module early: an option that cannot outperform a simpler mechanism has not justified itself.

**Record it even when rejected.** Move 3 requires what was rejected and what is lost by rejecting it. A rejected
alternative frequently returns later as the right answer for a segment.

---

# Where It Misleads

**Alternatives are included to be dismissed.** A one-line mention of "we could do this manually" followed by
rejection is the straw man `Solution-Exploration.md` prohibits. If it is in the option set, it gets scored properly.

**Simple solutions are undervalued because they are unexciting.** A template that removes 60% of a problem next week
is worth more than a system that removes 90% next year — and `06-business`'s time-to-first-customer criterion is
where that shows.

**The human option is rejected on margin without checking the price.** Services carry lower margin and much shorter
time to revenue. For a small operator that trade is often correct, and `01-idea`'s operator-goal question decides it.

**"Doing nothing" is never scored.** It is the status quo, it is what `05-competition` required as a competitor, and
it wins more often than product documents admit.

**An alternative is treated as a phase rather than an option.** "Start manual, then automate" is a legitimate
strategy and a different thing from a manual option — it needs the automation trigger stated, or it is two plans
stapled together.
