# Empathy Map

---

# What It Is

A structuring device that sorts what is known about a person into quadrants — what they say, think, do
and feel — usually with pains and gains alongside.

The framework treats it as a **structuring device with no output of its own.** Its legitimate use is
narrow but real: two of its quadrants are directly observable and two are not, and laying them side by
side makes the difference impossible to ignore.

| Quadrant | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **Does** | Observable. Strongest evidence — this is `Behaviors.md` |
| **Says** | Observable, but reported. Moderate at best |
| **Thinks** | Inferred. Always `[inferred]` or `[assumption]` |
| **Feels** | Inferred. Same, and the most likely to be projected |

Used that way, it is a tagging discipline. Used as an analysis, it manufactures interiority for a person
nobody has met.

---

# When It Applies

Optionally, during Move 3 (Embody), to organize existing research before writing the persona. Never as
a substitute for Move 4 (Observe).

---

# How to Apply It Here

**Fill Does first, and let it constrain the rest.** Anything in Thinks or Feels that does not follow
from something in Does is an invention. Working in that order turns the map into a check.

**Tag every quadrant separately.** The whole value is in the asymmetry: a map where Does is `[verified]`
and Feels is `[assumption]` is doing its job. A uniformly untagged map has hidden the only thing it was
good for.

**Use it to find the gaps, then discard it.** Empty quadrants are the useful output — they say what to
research next. The map itself does not need to appear in any artifact.

**Never let a Feels entry become a quote.** Inferred emotion written in the first person becomes
apparent primary evidence, which is the prohibition in `Personas.md` reached by a side door.

---

# Where It Misleads

**Two of its four quadrants cannot be observed, and the format gives all four equal weight.** This is
the structural objection, and it is the same one the framework makes about SWOT: a layout that levels
verified and invented content will be filled as though they were equivalent.

**Feels is where projection enters the framework.** "Overwhelmed", "anxious about compliance",
"frustrated by legacy tools" are the analyst's assumptions in the user's voice, and they are extremely
durable once written.

**Completing the map feels like understanding the user.** It is a rearrangement of what was already
known. If no new research went in, no new knowledge came out — but the artifact looks like it did.

**It has no priority and no cost.** A trivial irritation and a serious risk sit in the same quadrant at
the same size. `Pain-Points.md` and `04-problem` carry severity; the map cannot.
