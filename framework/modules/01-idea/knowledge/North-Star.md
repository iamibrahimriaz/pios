# North Star

---

# What It Is

The single measure that represents value delivered to the user — the one number a team uses to
resolve disagreements about whether things are getting better.

Its purpose is arbitration. That is why there is exactly one: two north stars resolve nothing,
they simply give each side of an argument a number to hold.

**This module nominates a candidate.** `12-metrics` ratifies it, defines it computably, sets its
target, asks how it would be gamed, and builds the counter-metrics. All of that requires
information module 01 does not have.

---

# When It Applies

In Pass 3 (Locate), as one line in the brief, tied to the user outcome the idea claims.

---

# How to Apply It Here

**Nominate the unit of value, not the unit of activity.** What does the user get, counted in
their terms?

| Activity — weak | Value — strong |
| --- | --- |
| Notes created | Consultations documented before the patient leaves |
| Logins | Clinics finished on time |
| Documents processed | Claims accepted first time |

**Check it could fall.** If the candidate can only rise — total users, cumulative records,
revenue to date — it measures elapsed time and cannot be steered by. Replace it with a rate or a
cohort measure. This is module 12's vanity check, applied a dozen modules early because it costs
nothing here.

**Do not choose revenue.** Revenue is a consequence of a north star. It measures what the company
received rather than what the user got, and it moves last.

**Leave the target alone.** A candidate north star with an invented target is worse than one
without, because the target is what gets quoted.

---

# Where It Misleads

**A north star chosen this early is chosen without knowing the user.** `03-user` may establish
that the valuable outcome is something else entirely — accuracy rather than speed, confidence
rather than time. Expect the candidate to change, and mark it as a candidate so that changing it
is not a reversal.

**One number cannot describe a product.** A north star arbitrates; it does not explain. That is
why module 12 pairs it with counter-metrics — without them, an organization improves its single
number and degrades the product in good faith.

**Nominating one early can narrow the research.** If the candidate is "time saved", it becomes
tempting to research time and not accuracy. Hold it loosely until module 12.
