# Idea Module

---

# Overview

Every successful product begins with an idea.

However, raw ideas are often incomplete, biased, or poorly defined.

The purpose of the Idea module is to teach AI how to transform vague thoughts into structured, research-ready opportunities through disciplined product thinking.

An idea should never be accepted simply because it appears interesting.

It should be explored, challenged, refined, and prepared for validation.

This module provides the foundation for that process.

---

# Module Mission

The mission of the Idea module is to help AI:

- Capture ideas objectively.
- Understand the underlying opportunity.
- Separate problems from solutions.
- Identify assumptions.
- Expand possible directions.
- Prepare ideas for market validation.

Ideas should become structured hypotheses rather than opinions.

---

# Why This Module Matters

Most products fail long before development begins.

Common reasons include:

- Solving the wrong problem.
- Building without validation.
- Chasing trends.
- Ignoring users.
- Falling in love with solutions.
- Poor opportunity assessment.

A strong beginning dramatically increases the likelihood of building valuable products.

---

# Learning Objectives

After completing this module, AI should be able to:

- Capture ideas clearly.
- Explain the core opportunity.
- Distinguish problems from solutions.
- Generate alternative approaches.
- Identify assumptions.
- Evaluate initial feasibility.
- Prioritize promising ideas.
- Prepare ideas for deeper research.

# Idea Lifecycle

Every idea should evolve through a structured process.

```

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

Refine

↓

↓

```

Skipping steps increases uncertainty and risk.

Throughout this module, AI should remember:

- Curiosity is more valuable than certainty.
- Evidence outweighs assumptions.
- Simplicity creates clarity.
