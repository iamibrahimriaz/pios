# Customer Acquisition

---

# What It Is

The route to the **first ten customers** — the reality test of the entire module.

> A business model with excellent arithmetic and no answer here is not a business model.

The requirement is specificity sufficient to act on tomorrow:

| A route | Not a route |
| --- | --- |
| "Solo practices in «region» who posted in «forum» about «problem»" | "Digital marketing" |
| "The four practices in my former partner's network" | "Word of mouth" |
| "Attendees of «named annual conference», approached in person" | "Content marketing and SEO" |
| "The 60 members of «professional association» in «county»" | "Social media presence" |

The gate requires this path to be described. It is the requirement that most often reveals a market nobody can
actually reach.

---

# When It Applies

In Entry 5 (Route). It also supplies the channel assumption CAC is derived from, so it precedes the economics
logically even though it is numbered after them.

---

# How to Apply It Here

**Name organizations or profiles specific enough to search for.** If the description cannot be turned into a list
of names by someone with a browser and a week, it is not specific enough.

**Record existing access first.** A relationship, a former employer, an audience, a professional network, a
credential that grants entry. Any of these changes CAC entirely, and the first ten customers almost always come
through one of them.

**Say plainly that the first ten do not scale.** Warm networks are the correct way to start and the wrong basis
for a CAC projection. `CAC.md` names this; `11-growth` requires a repeatable channel separately.

**Match the channel to where `03-user` observed the segment.** A channel chosen because it is familiar to the
operator, rather than because the segment is there, is the most common failure — and `11-growth` will require the
observation to be cited.

**State the sales motion and the cycle length.** Self-serve, founder-led, inside sales, or partner. Then how long
one deal takes: a nine-month cycle against twelve months of runway is a finding, and it belongs in
`Financial-Risks.md`.

---

# Where It Misleads

**Channel names substitute for routes.** "SEO" and "content marketing" describe activities, not access, and
neither identifies a single person who could buy this. The specificity test exists because the vague version is
always available.

**Existing access is treated as a channel.** Ten customers from a personal network prove the problem is real and
prove nothing about acquisition. The eleventh customer is where the business model actually begins.

**Institutional sales cycles are underestimated by a factor of two or three.** Procurement, information
governance, budget cycles and committee schedules are structural, and `03-user` will have found them if it asked
who signs.

**A large SOM masks an unreachable market.** Reachability is the constraint SAM was supposed to apply, and this
entry is the check on whether it really did. If nobody can name a route, SAM was optimistic.

**The route is written for the plan rather than tested.** The honest version includes what was tried and did not
work, and what access does not exist. Both are more useful than a channel list.
