# Communities Library

---

# Overview

Product Intelligence does not develop in isolation.

The most valuable insights often emerge through discussions, collaboration, shared experiences, and continuous engagement with professional communities.

The Communities library maintains a curated collection of trusted communities where professionals exchange ideas, publish research, solve problems, and discuss emerging trends.

This library helps Product Intelligence OS remain connected to the broader product ecosystem.

---

# Mission

The mission of this library is to identify and organize high-quality communities that contribute meaningful knowledge, practical experience, and continuous learning opportunities.

Communities should broaden perspective rather than reinforce assumptions.

---

# Why Communities Matter

Communities provide knowledge that traditional documentation often cannot.

Benefits include:

- Practical experience
- Industry trends
- Expert discussions
- Emerging technologies
- Product feedback
- Career insights
- Networking opportunities
- Real-world problem solving

Strong communities accelerate learning.

---

# Objectives

This library helps AI and contributors:

- Discover trusted communities.
- Stay informed about industry trends.
- Learn from experienced practitioners.
- Participate in professional discussions.
- Find authoritative experts.
- Identify emerging best practices.

---

# Selection Criteria

Communities should be selected based on:

- Credibility
- Professionalism
- Active participation
- Educational value
- Quality discussions
- Knowledge sharing
- Long-term sustainability

Popularity alone is not sufficient.

---

# Quality Guidelines

Communities included in this library should:

- Encourage respectful discussions.
- Promote evidence-based thinking.
- Welcome diverse perspectives.
- Maintain active moderation.
- Provide meaningful educational value.
- Demonstrate consistent activity.

Communities that primarily spread misinformation, low-quality content, or promotional material should not be included.

---

# Naming Convention

Use descriptive filenames.

Examples:

```
mind-the-product.md

product-coalition.md

ux-stack-exchange.md

hacker-news.md

reddit-product-management.md

product-school-community.md
```

Names should clearly identify the community.

# Usage Guidelines

- Discover new ideas.
- Validate assumptions.
- Learn industry practices.
- Monitor emerging trends.
- Identify useful discussions.
- Expand professional knowledge.

Community discussions should complement—not replace—structured research and verified evidence.
